Our story

From recovery audits to a platform that prevents the problems in the first place

Where we started

We started in accounts payable consulting. Recovery audits, process analysis, solution implementations. Over 15 years, we worked with hundreds of clients across industries and geographies. We analyzed their AP processes, recommended tools, and helped them implement those tools. And then we watched the same problems come back.

What we kept seeing

The tools were fragmented. Reconciliation in one product, duplicate detection in another, invoice processing in a third. Each had its own login, its own data, and its own limitations. Cross-referencing anything meant exporting to spreadsheets. Reporting meant stitching data together by hand.

The turning point

We tried to make existing solutions work. We pushed vendors for better integration. We built workarounds. Eventually, we decided it would be faster to build what we always wanted than to keep waiting for someone else to do it.

360° AP launches

One platform, one login, five modules that share a single data layer. Everything we learned in 15 years of AP consulting is built into how it works.

What drives us

The principles behind the platform

Completeness

The name says it. 360 degrees. The platform covers the full AP lifecycle because we saw what happens when it doesn't. Gaps between tools create blind spots, and blind spots cost money. We built for complete coverage from day one.

Precision

Accounts payable is a function where errors have direct financial consequences. Up to 99%+ document processing accuracy is not a marketing number. It is the standard we hold the platform to. From data extraction to duplicate detection to reconciliation matching, precision is built into every process.

Partnership

We charge nothing for implementation. We run the proof of concept with your actual data. We stay involved after launch. Our success is measured by your outcomes, not by hours billed.

Agility

A focused team that moves fast. In product development, in implementation, and in responding to what our clients need. We do not have layers of bureaucracy between a client request and a platform update. That speed is a structural advantage, and we intend to keep it.
