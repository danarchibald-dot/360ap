AI-powered '360° AP Accurate' solution now available in Coupa App Marketplace

AP Accurate is now available in the Coupa App Marketplace, extending Coupa's platform and increasing financial controls, on-time supplier payments, and reduced supplier queries.

London, United Kingdom, March 2026

360° AP (360-degree Accounts Payable), a leading provider of AI-powered accounts payable automation solutions, today announced the release of AP Accurate, its flagship AI-powered engine and core component of its 360° AP suite, into the Coupa App Marketplace - connecting businesses with certified, pre-built solutions.

The Coupa Total Spend Management Platform helps teams collaborate to build more agile and sustainable operations, delivering intelligent and responsible spend strategies to meet their company's purpose.

What AP Accurate delivers

AP Accurate delivers fully touchless supplier statement reconciliations, enabling suppliers to email both PDF and Excel statements to a dedicated address. Its intelligent document processing (IDP) engine then extracts and imports the data within 12 hours, automatically matching statements against Coupa via API.

The intuitive reconciliation cockpit provides real-time visibility, automated vendor follow-ups, KPI dashboards with Supplier Health Scorecards and workflows that ensure no credit or discount is ever missed.

What Coupa says

With invoice volumes rising rapidly and reconciliation errors threatening cash flow and supplier relationships, true AI-powered precision in accounts payable has become essential for finance teams worldwide. We're proud to have 360° AP on the Coupa App Marketplace, giving our customers even greater efficiency in accounts payable and allowing them to achieve faster month-end closes, along with stronger financial controls.

Eric Washer, SVP of Product Strategy at Coupa

Certified Coupa App Marketplace solution

As a certified Coupa App Marketplace solution, 360° AP - AP Accurate meets the requirements established by Coupa through its App Marketplace Partner Program. The program enables software partners to build complementary solutions that easily connect into the Coupa platform. Customers can benefit by extending the capabilities of their spend management platform, solving unique business challenges, while reducing the cost of third-party software integrations.

Connecting AP Accurate into the Coupa Total Spend Management platform gives our customers a streamlined, touchless supplier statement reconciliations, which in return gives an accurate accounts payable balance, less supplier queries and enhanced compliance. We're thrilled to be part of the Coupa App Marketplace and recognised as a trusted app provider and look forward to developing our relationship with Coupa to help customers transform their AP operations.

Werner van Dalen, spokesperson for 360° AP

About 360° AP

360° AP integrates five AI-driven apps - AP Automate (automation for supplier invoices and credit notes), AP Assure (duplicate payment detection and prevention), AP Analyze (BI dashboards and reports), AP Assist (supplier query management) and AP Accurate (supplier statement reconciliation) - to automate, assure, analyze, assist, and accurately manage accounts payable processes end-to-end.

It empowers finance teams to eliminate costly errors, strengthen financial controls, capture every credit or discount, ensure on-time supplier payments and achieve faster month-end closes with complete accuracy and real-time visibility.

To learn more about how your organisation can collaborate with AP Accurate and Coupa, visit: marketplace.coupa.com/en-US/apps/786659
