1. Introduction

AP 360 Limited ("we", "us", "our", or "360° AP") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal information that we collect, process, and store in connection with our accounts payable automation platform and website located at www.360-ap.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, request a demo, or use our services. It also describes your rights and choices regarding that information.

360° AP operates globally and is subject to multiple data protection frameworks, including:

  • The Gibraltar Data Protection Act 2004 (as amended), aligned with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and UK GDPR
  • The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) - South Africa
  • Applicable United States privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and other state privacy laws where applicable

By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

As 360° AP processes personal data of individuals in Gibraltar and the EU, we act as a Data Controller (and in some instances, as a Data Processor on behalf of enterprise clients) under the applicable regulations.

3. Personal information we collect

We collect personal information in the following categories:

3.1 Website visitor data

  • IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time and date of visit, referring URL, and session duration
  • Cookie identifiers and analytics data (see Section 8 - Cookies)

3.2 Enquiry and demo request data

  • Full name and business email address
  • Company name, job title, and telephone number
  • Information submitted via our contact form or Calendly demo booking tool
  • Communications history with our sales and support teams

3.3 Client platform data

  • Business contact details of authorised platform users (name, email, role)
  • Login credentials (stored in encrypted form)
  • Financial data submitted to or generated by the platform (invoices, payment records, ERP transaction data)
  • Usage logs and audit trails within the platform

3.4 Supplier / vendor data (processed on behalf of clients)

When enterprise clients use our platform, we may process personal data relating to their suppliers and vendors as a Data Processor. This may include:

  • Supplier contact names and email addresses
  • Bank account and payment information
  • Invoice and statement records

In these cases, our clients are the Data Controllers and we process this data solely on their documented instructions.

4. How we use your personal information

We use personal information for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

To operate and deliver our services - including onboarding, platform access, ERP integration, and customer support. Legal basis: Contract performance; Legitimate interests.

To respond to enquiries and schedule demos - including processing contact form submissions and Calendly bookings. Legal basis: Consent; Legitimate interests.

To improve our website and platform - using analytics to understand usage patterns and improve user experience. Legal basis: Legitimate interests; Consent (where cookies are used).

To send marketing communications - only where you have provided consent, or where permitted under the soft opt-in rule (existing clients). You may opt out at any time. Legal basis: Consent; Legitimate interests.

To comply with legal obligations - including regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and audit requirements. Legal basis: Legal obligation.

To protect our legitimate interests - including security monitoring, detecting misuse, and enforcing our terms of service. Legal basis: Legitimate interests.

5. How we share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

5.1 Service providers and sub-processors

  • Google LLC - analytics and tracking (Google Analytics)
  • HubSpot / Salesforce - CRM and marketing automation
  • Calendly LLC - demo scheduling
  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting providers (e.g., AWS, Azure, or equivalent)
  • ERP integration partners (SAP, Oracle, Coupa, NetSuite) as required to deliver our services

All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements and are required to maintain appropriate security standards.

5.2 Legal and regulatory disclosures

We may disclose personal information where required by law, court order, or regulatory authority, including to comply with requests from law enforcement or government agencies in Gibraltar, South Africa, the EU/UK, or the United States.

5.3 Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

6. International data transfers

360° AP operates across multiple jurisdictions. Your personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including Gibraltar, South Africa, and the United States.

Where we transfer personal data from the EEA, UK, or South Africa to third countries, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs)
  • Binding Corporate Rules or other approved transfer mechanisms
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable

For transfers involving South African data subjects, we comply with Section 72 of POPIA and ensure that the recipient country provides an adequate level of protection.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Our general retention periods are:

  • Website visitor analytics data: 26 months (Google Analytics default)
  • Enquiry and demo request data: 3 years from last contact
  • Client platform data: Duration of the contract plus 7 years (for audit and legal compliance purposes)
  • Supplier/vendor data processed on behalf of clients: As directed by the client and set out in the Data Processing Agreement
  • Marketing contact data: Until consent is withdrawn or opt-out is requested

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised in accordance with our data destruction policy.

8. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and collect analytics data. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies - required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies - including Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use our site
  • Marketing / preference cookies - to remember your settings and, where consented, serve relevant content

You can control cookies through your browser settings or by using a cookie consent management tool on our website. Where required by law (e.g., under GDPR or POPIA), we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

For more information about how Google uses data collected via Google Analytics, please visit: policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

9.1 Rights under GDPR / Gibraltar DPA (EU, UK, Gibraltar residents)

  • Right of access - to obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification - to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling

9.2 Rights under POPIA (South African residents)

  • Right to be notified that your personal information is being collected
  • Right to access your personal information
  • Right to request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

9.3 Rights under US privacy laws (California and other US residents)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Right to delete personal information (subject to exceptions)
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights
  • Right to limit use and disclosure of sensitive personal information

Residents of other US states with applicable privacy laws (including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and others) may have similar rights. We will respond to verifiable requests in accordance with applicable law.

9.4 Exercising your rights

To exercise any of your privacy rights, please contact us at sales@360-ap.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request." We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally 30 days, extendable where permitted). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Secure cloud infrastructure and ERP integration protocols
  • Staff training on data protection and information security

While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected individuals and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

12. Third-party websites and services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including Calendly for demo scheduling. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you access through our website.

13. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or a prominent notice on our website.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

14. Complaints and supervisory authorities

If you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority:

  • Gibraltar: Gibraltar Regulatory Authority (GRA) - www.gra.gi
  • EU: Your national data protection authority (a list is available at edpb.europa.eu)
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) - ico.org.uk
  • South Africa: Information Regulator - inforegulator.org.za
  • USA: Federal Trade Commission (FTC) or your state Attorney General

We encourage you to contact us first so that we may address your concerns directly.
